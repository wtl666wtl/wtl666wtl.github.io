Tianle Wang 王天乐

I am a Ph.D. student at Purdue University, advised by Prof. Abulhair Saparov. Previously, I received my M.S. from UC San Diego, advised by Prof. Jingbo Shang, and my B.E. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I was part of the ACM Honors Class.

My research focuses on reasoning and planning in large language models. In particular, I explore how reinforcement learning during post-training can improve the reasoning capabilities of foundation models.
